With 1,370 faculty members, 88 UG programs, 55 PG programs, and deep industry ties through 300+ MoUs, JAIN University provides world-class education that makes graduates industry-ready from day one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before attending Spot Counselling.

Spot Counselling is an opportunity to meet JAIN University representatives directly in your city. You can discuss course options, career paths, get your documents verified, and if you meet the eligibility criteria, receive an on-the-spot admission offer the same day.
Bring your 10th and 12th mark sheets, transfer certificate, character certificate, government ID (Aadhaar/passport), 4 passport-sized photographs, and entrance exam scorecards (JEE/NEET/CAT etc.) if applicable. Carry originals + 2 sets of photocopies.
Yes. JAIN University offers merit scholarships (Medha, Eklavya etc.) for qualifying students. Our team at the venue will assess your academic profile on-the-spot and guide you on scholarships available for your marks, category, and chosen program.
You can still apply online at jainuniversity.ac.in. However, attending Spot Counselling gives you significant advantages — personalised guidance, instant document verification, and on-the-spot admission decisions that can save weeks of effort.
Walk-ins are welcome, but registering in advance is strongly recommended. It allows our team to prepare your profile beforehand, assign you a priority slot, and ensure you receive dedicated time with a counsellor without long queues.
JAIN University conducts its own Jain Entrance Test (JET). It also accepts JEE Main and CUET scores for B.Tech, CAT/MAT/CMAT for MBA, CLAT for Law programs, and GATE/KPGCET for M.Tech. Strong scores in these exams may also help you qualify for scholarships.
